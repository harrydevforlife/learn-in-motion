Install on iPhone

  1. 1Open this page in Safari
  2. 2Tap Share in the toolbar
  3. 3Scroll down, choose Add to Home Screen, then Add

It opens full screen like a normal app. Heads-up: the Home Screen app keeps its own saved items, separate from Safari. Start saving after you install, or move items over with Export and Import.
